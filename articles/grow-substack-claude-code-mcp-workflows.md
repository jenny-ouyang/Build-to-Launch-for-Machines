## Summary

Growing a Substack is mostly tracking work. Triaging an inbox, sizing a niche, studying who is winning, drafting notes, scheduling them, reading the numbers afterward — all repeatable, all trackable, and all the part that gets dropped first when life gets busy. This recorded Build to Launch workshop applies the 80/20 rule to that reality: hand the mechanical 80% to Claude and keep the 20% that needs judgment and voice. The frame is three stages every Substack writer moves through — read and learn, build your own publication, grow toward monetization — and an engine that meets you wherever you are standing.

The whole system runs on one connector: a single MCP hosted on Quick Viral Notes that links Claude to Substack data, included in the paid Build to Launch subscription. Setup is a two-minute, on-camera flow — register with your subscriber email, connect your publication, add a Chrome extension, add the MCP to your connectors. Under the hood it holds roughly 120,000 active newsletters, re-scraped weekly so the competitive data stays current. That database is the fuel for all eight workflows.

The eight workflows split by stage. **Subscription Brief** collapses a 40-to-100-post morning inbox into a few themed reads and a "read these two first" line. **Landscape Explorer** sizes a niche before you commit — real subscriber counts, paid percentage, and a go/caution/crowded verdict (marketing came back with 6,000+ newsletters, ~30% paid). **Creator Study** reverse-engineers a top creator's public playbook: cadence, notes rhythm, and SEO habits like Substack's overridable title, subtitle, and slug. **Notes Engine** turns one long post into a batch of notes in Jenny's voice, run through a voice gate against a hooks library before anything ships. **Scheduler** queues those notes on a cadence behind a confirm gate — the only workflow that writes to the live account.

The measurement half closes the loop. **Notes Performance** ranks shipped notes on real reach, not likes, and surfaces the "utility winner" — low likes, high clicks (her last 200 notes pulled 17,935 clicks and 317 free subscribers). **Posts Performance** ranks articles on conversion, adds a Google Search Console traffic read, and hands back next-article ideas each traced to a post that already converted (one pillar post behind the paywall brings in one to two paid subscribers). **Subscriber Study** reads the list you already own to show who converted and why — the finding that paid subscribers came in through MCP how-to content and the Practical AI Builder program reshaped what Jenny prioritizes. Winners feed straight back into the Notes Engine as next week's seeds, so measurement becomes the seed for the next round of writing.

The session stays honest about live tooling: when the MCP request hung mid-call, Jenny debugged it on camera — isolating whether Claude or the hosted endpoint was the problem by scheduling a single note and verifying it landed in Substack's native scheduler, then triggering a background sync and moving on. The throughline: the model is a commodity, the harness is yours, and the engine starts with one connection and one workflow — the Subscription Brief — run this week.
